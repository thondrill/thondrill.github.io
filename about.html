Research & Work

I am a researcher and creative technologist based in Chicago. Currently, I am a Graduate Research Assistant at PUSH Studio and an M.S. student in Human-Computer Interaction at DePaul University.

At PUSH Studio, I am developing a system that uses non-invasive brain-computer interfaces (BCIs) to create physical art — curious about the possibilities for accessibility and the novel ways we can produce and experience art together.

Prior to this, I worked as a Systems Engineer at Bank of America. My undergraduate research at The College of New Jersey focused on designing VR wayfinding tools with haptic feedback for visually impaired individuals. I also co-developed the puzzle platformer Mason & Strings, published on Steam and showcased at the TooManyGames indie showcase.

Outside the Lab

Outside the lab, I am a two-time marathon runner and triathlete. I'm currently training for my third, which will be the London Marathon in April of next year.

While I'm not training, you can find me with my two cats, Barry and Ouija. I'm fluent in Tamil and English, and I'm currently working on learning French and Hindi!
