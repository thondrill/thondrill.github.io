DePaul University · Indiana University Bloomington · HCI Research · 2025 – Present

Toward the Design of a Transmasc Sex Ed Tool

Jacob Boss, Mel Monier, Thendral Prabu Advisor: Patrick Shih

Building on survey findings from 52 transmasculine participants about how they seek sexual education—where participants reported low trust in school curricula and medical practitioners, relying instead on peers, romantic partners, and online media—this follow-up study conducts an Asynchronous Remote Community (ARC) with 20 transmasculine participants through Microsoft Teams. The study explores their experiences, perspectives, and design preferences to inform the design of a platform that combines educational rigor with community perspectives and reflects transmasculine bodies and experiences.

Undergraduate Research · TCNJ · 2021 – 2022

VR Navigation Training: Haptic Sensory Feedback for Non-Visual Wayfinding

Thendral Prabu (Lead Developer & Researcher), Sharif Mohammad Shahnewaz Ferdous (Faculty Advisor)

Indoor environments present significant navigational barriers for visually impaired people, who frequently lack safe tools to explore unfamiliar layouts. Engineered an accessible virtual reality training environment paired with custom Arduino hardware driving eccentric rotating mass (ERM) vibration motors. Users explore virtual architectural floorplans via tactile distance feedback and spatialized audio, safely building durable cognitive maps prior to visiting physical spaces.

Virtual Reality Arduino / Physical Computing Haptic Actuators Spatial Cognitive Mapping Unity (C#)

DePaul University HCI · 2025

CTA Transit App: Accessible Urban Wayfinding & System Transparency

Thendral Prabu, UX Research & Design Team

A user-centered redesign of the Chicago Transit Authority (CTA) mobile experience addressing critical accessibility gaps for riders with mobility and visual impairments. Conducted rider interviews to identify high-stress failure points, implementing prominent real-time elevator outage indicators, detour guidance, "ghost bus" tracking transparency, and WCAG 2.2 AAA high-contrast route planners.
